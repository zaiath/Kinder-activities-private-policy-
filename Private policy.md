## KinderAI — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [04 SEP 2026]
**Last Updated:** [04 SEP 2026]

Thank you for using **KinderAI** ("the App"), an early-learning app for young children made by **CodeArter** ("we", "us", "our"). Your family's privacy is very important to us.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices parents and guardians have.

### 1. We Collect Very Little Information

We designed KinderAI to work **offline** — most learning content is stored directly on the device and does not require an internet connection or an account.

**We do NOT require:**
- An account or registration
- An email address
- Your child's name, photo, or personal details (parents may enter a nickname that stays only on the device)

### 2. Information That MAY Be Collected Automatically

The only internet activity in the App is served by **Google AdMob**, which shows advertising. When ads are shown, Google may collect limited, non-personal data such as:
- Device type and operating system
- General (non-precise) location
- Advertising identifiers
- Usage data for the ads shown

This data is used by Google to show age-appropriate ads and to measure performance. It is **not** shared with us in a way that identifies your child.



### 3. What We Do NOT Do
- We do **not** sell personal information.
- We do **not** use personal information to build profiles of children.
- We do **not** allow third parties to collect personal information from children for their own purposes.
- We do **not** require permission-based features (camera, microphone, contacts, location) for the core app.

### 4. Third-Party Services

**Google AdMob** is the sole third-party service used. You can read Google's own child-safety practices here:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Families & Ads: https://families.google/families/

### 5. Parents' Rights and Controls
As a parent or guardian, you can:
- Disable ads / uninstall the App at any time.
- Reset or delete the advertising identifier on your device (Settings → Google → Ads).
- Contact us to ask what data, if any, is held and to request deletion.

### 6. Data Retention & Security
We do not store your child's content on our servers. Any on-device data (such as a nickname or coloring saves) stays on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

### 7. Children's Privacy Laws (Worldwide)
We operate in line with:
- **United States** — COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- **European Union** — GDPR & GDPR-K (for children under 16 / per member state)
- **United Kingdom** — UK GDPR & Age Appropriate Design Code
- **Brazil** — LGPD
- **Other regions** — general child-safety best practices

### 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
